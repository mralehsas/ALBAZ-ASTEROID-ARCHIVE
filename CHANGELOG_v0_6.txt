Asteroid Archive v0.6 — Advanced Comparison Lab
================================================

NEW
---
- Rebuilt the comparison view as an independent scientific laboratory.
- Compare up to four objects.
- Add objects directly by designation, number or name through JPL SBDB.
- Continue supporting selection from the close-approach table.
- Automatic SBDB enrichment for selected close-approach records.
- Side-by-side scientific matrix covering identity, encounter, physical and orbital fields.
- Published/estimated/unavailable quality labels for every comparison field.
- Density scenarios: carbonaceous, stony, dense stony, iron and custom.
- Estimated mass and kinetic energy with explicit assumptions.
- Relative bar charts for distance, velocity, diameter, mass, energy and MOID.
- Comparison insights: closest, fastest, largest, highest estimated energy, lowest MOID, PHA count and data gaps.
- Dedicated HTML comparison report and CSV export support.
- Quick-selection examples based on the bundled close-approach reference set.

FIXED
-----
- Corrected NASA updater numeric record-limit fields: any integer is now valid.
- Added a global hidden-element rule so hidden panels and custom-density fields do not remain visible.
- Corrected the inspector comparison action label.
- Preserved local close-approach comparison when SBDB is temporarily unavailable.

SCIENTIFIC POLICY
-----------------
- The comparison does not claim to predict impacts.
- Encounter velocity is not labelled as atmospheric impact velocity.
- Mass and energy are explicitly marked as estimates.
- Missing values remain unavailable.
